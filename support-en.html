Features

  • Open scores: all three MusicXML formats (.xml, .musicxml, compressed .mxl), plus vector PDFs

exported by notation software (experimental)

  • Real engraving: the Bravura music font draws noteheads, stems, beams, slurs, ornaments, chord symbols,

repeats and multi-voice rests to engraving conventions

  • Sound out of the box: a bundled GM sound bank performs dynamics, pedals, staccato, fermatas,

trills / turns / mordents and grace notes — no download needed

  • Transport: return to start, play / pause / resume (resume picks up exactly where it stopped);

select a note first and playback begins at that measure

  • Note details: tap any note to see pitch, duration, voice and staff, position, articulations, dynamics,

lyrics and chord symbols. The score is read-only — nothing is changed by accident

  • Repeats done properly: repeat barlines, voltas, D.C. / D.S. al Fine and To Coda are followed;

the play cursor tracks the printed page, rests and tied notes included

  • Practice: scales in any key (up / down, 1–3 octaves, optional cadence written the way graded tutors

do it), plus metronome, tempo, loop, swing and a piano roll

  • Two built-in example scores: G major (3 octaves) and A harmonic minor (2 octaves) with cadences,

shipped as the original MusicXML files

  • Export: PDF / PNG / MIDI / WAV / MusicXML through the system share sheet
  • Chinese and English interface, completely offline

Support

Frequently asked:

  • Durations look wrong after importing a PDF? Vector-PDF import is experimental: staves, pitches and

measures are recovered accurately, durations are approximate — please check after import.

  • Can I edit the score? No — the app is read-only by design (reading, listening and practising).

The settings in the Details panel are playback settings (instrument, mute, mixer, transpose).

  • No sound? Playback uses the GM sound bank bundled with the app (no download needed).

If it is still silent, check the device volume and the ring / silent switch.

  • Which formats are supported? MusicXML (.xml, .musicxml), compressed MusicXML (.mxl) and

vector PDF. This version has no image-based OMR and does not import MIDI.
