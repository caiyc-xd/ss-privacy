Version history

1.1 (first update · build 1)

New features

  • Jianpu view (numbered notation, experimental): switch any score to numbered notation with one tap — melody only by default, or chord-stacked; it shares the same score data as the staff view and switching never modifies your score
  • Coverage-score practice (experimental): four single-theme scores that drill repeats and jumps — repeats & endings / measure & beat repeats / D.S. & Coda / D.C. & Fine; the first-pass false jump at To Coda is fixed
  • Score library: scores you open are collected automatically, with favourites, rename and delete
  • More practice: four new exercise types — intervals, rhythm patterns, chord progressions and sight-singing; short arpeggios now respect octave and direction and use the inverted-position window pattern that graded syllabuses use (root position → first inversion → second inversion …)
  • Settings: page-turn options (swipe / Bluetooth pedal or keyboard / half page), a Pencil annotation layer, Bluetooth MIDI output, links and acknowledgements; the About row now shows the version and build number
  • Shortcuts: open your most recent score with Shortcuts or Siri (no longer Chinese-only on English devices)
  • Safer edits: transposing and brace changes now ask for confirmation

Playback

  • Fixed "pressing Resume after Pause started from the beginning again"; resuming after switching scores no longer plays the previous score
  • The play cursor now runs on a single timeline and follows the notated position through rests and ties
  • Tempo marks play and display in the score's own units
  • On iPhone the score and cursor no longer jump up and down on the last line of a non-final page

Engraving and marks

  • Beams: no longer overlap noteheads, and a second beam no longer stops short of the stem; beam groups split at a 2-octave pitch span, so stems no longer cross
  • Line breaking and spacing: a measure that cannot fit on one system now breaks inside the measure and continues on the next line (dense measures are no longer squeezed together); noteheads follow a minimum-ink-clearance spacing and no longer overlap; a measure that is only slightly over-wide is still compressed rather than given a line of its own
  • Clef and time-signature space: a mid-measure clef change or time signature now reserves the space it actually needs and no longer sits on the notes; the "scrambled clefs" regression on real scores is fixed
  • Mark placement: dynamics and crescendo/diminuendo wedges moved below the staff with full spans; octave marks sit outside the staff with their endpoints aligned; tempo glyphs and text share a baseline; Segno / Coda sit at the start of a measure and Fine / To Coda / D.C. / D.S. at its end; crescendo/diminuendo directions are no longer reversed; trills, mordents, turns and marcato (^) always sit above the staff
  • Mis-drawn marks: a batch fixed (Segno / D.S. / D.C. drew other marks, tempo marks showed the wrong note value, the pedal release mark, swapped staccato / tenuto icons, the arpeggio wiggle, the 15mb / 8va bass clefs, and more)
  • Mordents and ornaments: glyphs corrected and unified; the details panel's Chinese labels now match the glyph and the explanation; trill / turn / inverted-turn / upper- and lower-mordent terminology unified

Notation and MusicXML

  • When a voice in a multi-voice measure is shorter than the time signature, the added rests no longer lose their dots
  • Imported MusicXML without note-value tags infers the value from the actual duration (more accurate playback)
  • Every voice now fills its measure (no more "total duration does not match the time signature")
  • The long black line at the end of a score turned out to be a malformed multi-measure rest and is now drawn properly
  • Exported MusicXML no longer violates the schema (86 issues previously, all fixed)
  • Roman-numeral chords and inversions (with super/subscript figures) are parsed, displayed and exported
  • Hidden notes, other-dynamics, tuplet ratios and open-string notation are handled; a crescendo that spans measures no longer loses its endpoint
  • Better compatibility with scores exported by newer notation software

Demo score

  • The bundled "Demo March" now carries staccato, accents, tenuto, slurs, ties, a mordent, a triplet, a crescendo and a fermata, so "Note details → Music classroom" has something to explain

Size and performance

  • The sound bank is now a compressed .sf3 (about 13.9 MB, decoded on device on first launch) and the download is much smaller
  • Changing playback settings no longer re-lays out the whole score; concert pitch and the piano roll are cached so following the cursor costs less battery

Terminology and copy

  • Aligned with standard notation wording ("chord symbol" and friends); "coverage score" replaces the old name; missing English copy added
  • Voluntary donations: the support page (reachable from Settings → links) lists the developer's email — entirely optional; no payment of any kind in the app
  • Jianpu and the coverage score are labelled experimental

1.0 (production version · build 8)

  • Open and play instantly: MusicXML (.xml, .musicxml, .mxl) and vector PDFs exported by notation software
  • Bravura engraving: noteheads, stems, beams, slurs, ornaments, chord symbols, repeats and multi-voice rests
  • Offline playback with a bundled GM sound bank: dynamics, pedals, staccato, fermatas, trills / turns / mordents and grace notes are performed as written
  • Transport: return to start, play / pause / resume, and play from a selected note
  • Note details and the “Music classroom”: pitch, duration, voice and staff, articulations, dynamics, lyrics and chord symbols
  • Repeats and jumps: repeat barlines, voltas, D.C. / D.S. al Fine, To Coda
  • Practice: scales, arpeggios and chords in C / F / G and their relative minors, plus metronome, tempo, loop, swing and a piano roll
  • Export to PDF / PNG / MIDI / WAV / MusicXML; Chinese and English; fully offline — no account, no ads, no purchases
