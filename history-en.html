Version history

1.1 build 1 (upcoming · in development)

  • Playback: resuming after switching scores no longer plays the previous score; a single playback instance

(fixes the real-device "two sounds at once")

  • Engraving: beams no longer overlap noteheads, beam groups are split at a 2-octave pitch span (no more

crossed stems), and a mid-measure time signature reserves space by its measured glyph width

  • MusicXML: Roman-numeral chords and inversions (<function>, with super/subscript figures) are parsed,

displayed and exported; the exporter's schema violations are cleared (dacapo attribute name,

<measure-repeat> instead of the invalid <percent>, notations element order …); all six scores now

round-trip byte-identically on the first pass

  • Terminology aligned with MuseScore's Chinese wording («和弦记号» …), English added for the Shortcuts intents
  • The sound bank is now a compressed .sf3 (≈14 MB, decoded locally on first launch): the download drops

from ≈134 MB to ≈13 MB

  • Experimental features are labelled as such (jianpu, coverage score)
  • Requires iOS 16.0+, iPhone / iPad

1.0 build 8 (previous production)

  • First public release: open MusicXML (.xml/.musicxml/.mxl) and vector PDFs, Bravura engraving, a bundled GM

sound bank for offline playback, transport (return to start / pause & resume / play from the selected note),

note details + music classroom, metronome / tempo / loop / swing, scale practice and a piano roll,

export to PDF / PNG / MIDI / WAV / MusicXML, Chinese and English, fully offline

  • The bundled GM sound bank was the uncompressed .sf2 (≈148 MB), which is why the download was large
  • Build history: build 6 was submitted and withdrawn; builds 7 / 8 were archived but never uploaded
