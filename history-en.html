Version history

1.1 (in development)

  • Playback: fixed "pressing Resume after Pause started from the beginning again"; resuming after switching scores no longer plays the previous score; only one playback instance at a time
  • Engraving: beams no longer overlap noteheads; beam groups split at a 2-octave pitch span (no more crossed stems); a mid-measure time signature reserves space by its measured glyph width; fixed a batch of wrong music-font glyphs (Segno / D.S. / D.C. drew other marks, tempo marks showed the wrong note value, the pedal release mark, swapped staccato / tenuto icons, the arpeggio wiggle and the 15mb/8va bass clefs)
  • Mordents: glyphs settled against MuseScore renders (<mordent> has the vertical stroke, <inverted-mordent> does not) and the details panel's Chinese labels now match the glyph and the explanation
  • MusicXML: Roman-numeral chords and inversions (<function>, with super/subscript figures) are parsed, displayed and exported; exported files no longer violate the schema; hidden notes (print-object="no"), <other-dynamics>, tuplet <normal-type> and open-string notation are handled; a crescendo that spans measures no longer loses its endpoint
  • Demo score: the bundled “Demo March” now carries staccato, accents, tenuto, slurs, ties, a mordent, a triplet, a crescendo and a fermata, so “Note details → Music classroom” has something to explain
  • Practice: the repeats score was redesigned into four single-theme scores (repeats & endings / measure & beat repeats / D.S. & Coda / D.C. & Fine)
  • Terminology: aligned with MuseScore's Chinese wording; “coverage score” replaces the old name; English added for the Shortcuts intents
  • Download size: the sound bank is now a compressed .sf3 decoded locally on first launch — the download drops from about 134 MB to about 13 MB
  • Jianpu and the coverage score are labelled experimental
  • Requires iOS 16.0+, iPhone / iPad

1.0 (current release)

  • Open and play instantly: MusicXML (.xml, .musicxml, .mxl) and vector PDFs exported by notation software
  • Bravura engraving: noteheads, stems, beams, slurs, ornaments, chord symbols, repeats and multi-voice rests
  • Offline playback with a bundled GM sound bank: dynamics, pedals, staccato, fermatas, trills / turns / mordents and grace notes are performed as written
  • Transport: return to start, play / pause / resume, and play from a selected note
  • Note details and the “Music classroom”: pitch, duration, voice and staff, articulations, dynamics, lyrics and chord symbols
  • Repeats and jumps: repeat barlines, voltas, D.C. / D.S. al Fine, To Coda
  • Practice: scales, arpeggios and chords in C / F / G and their relative minors, plus metronome, tempo, loop, swing and a piano roll
  • Export to PDF / PNG / MIDI / WAV / MusicXML; Chinese and English; fully offline — no account, no ads, no purchases
